What is share and collaborate?

Share and collaborate in OpenPanel is how you get the right data to the right people without opening the door to everything.

Unlimited members - There’s no cap on how many people you can invite. Add your whole team, contractors, or stakeholders and manage them from a single members view.

Flexible access - Each member can have:

  • Organization-wide access - full visibility across all projects, ideal for admins and core team
  • Fine-grained access - access only to specific projects you choose, so stakeholders see only what’s relevant

What you can share - You’re not limited to "all or nothing" views:

  • Complete dashboards - share an entire dashboard so others see the same layout and reports you use
  • Individual reports - share a single report (e.g. one funnel or one retention chart) for focused updates

How you share - Every shared dashboard or report can be:

  • Publicly available - anyone with the link can view it (handy for status pages, exec summaries, or embedded views)
  • Password-protected - only people with the password can open the link, so you keep sensitive data under control

Inviting is straightforward: add members by email, assign access (org or specific projects), and optionally share dashboards or reports with public or password-protected links. Your team stays aligned on the same numbers without compromising security or clarity.

How sharing and collaboration works

Invite members, set access, then share dashboards or reports as needed.

1

Invite members

Add team members by email from the members area. They receive an invite and can sign in to OpenPanel with the access you’ve assigned.

2

Set access level

Give each member organization-wide access (all projects) or fine-grained access to specific projects only. You can change this anytime.

3

Share dashboards or reports

From any dashboard or report, use Share to generate a link. You can share a full dashboard or a single report.

4

Choose public or password-protected

Make the link public (anyone with the link can view) or set a password so only people who have it can open the shared view.

How teams use share and collaborate

From internal alignment to client-facing reports - sharing and access control fit many workflows.

Internal team alignment

Invite product, growth, and support with access to the projects they need. Share a main dashboard so everyone looks at the same KPIs in meetings.

Stakeholder and exec updates

Give execs or stakeholders limited access to specific projects and share a single report or dashboard. No need to give them the full product - just the view that matters.

Client and partner reports

Share a dashboard or report with a client or partner via link. Use a password when the data is sensitive so only authorized people can view it.

Public status or metrics pages

Share a dashboard publicly and embed or link it from your status page, docs, or marketing site so visitors see live metrics without signing in.
